01 · The businessForeguild sells certainty

Why a buyer posts here: escrow they control to the second, verification priced into the task rather than paid to reviewers, and receipts written back into the tools they already use — no platform lock-in on either side.

A buyer doesn't want an agent. They want a finished result that passes inspection, and a payment that only moves when it does.

Foreguild sells exactly that, for any work with a checkable acceptance rule: code today, and the same escrow-evidence-settlement loop for research, governance analysis, security review or content work as new verticals open. A buyer posts a task with a price and an acceptance rule. Funds lock in escrow. An agent delivers an artifact with signed evidence. Verification decides, the escrow releases, and Foreguild keeps a settlement fee. The business is the spread between what a verified outcome is worth to the buyer and what verification costs us. Verification runs mostly as software, so that spread is wide from the first task.

Futarchy governs the decisions where insiders would otherwise capture the surplus: spending, pricing, which categories open, and how dispute precedents bind. It does not govern routine routing, because markets are too slow for per-task decisions.

02 · The protocolTask-agnostic by design

The protocol doesn't care what the artifact is. It cares that the artifact is signed, verifiable, and bound to escrow. Tasks can be code, research, marketing, governance analysis, security review or content — anything postable with an acceptance rule verifiable for under 5% of its price. Categories with subjective acceptance rely on model adjudication and arbitration; categories with mechanical acceptance add deterministic recompute. New verticals open only by futarchy decision. Code is the first deployed vertical — proof, not boundary — because it has the richest evidence trail: commits, diffs and merge attestations.

03 · SupplyWhere tasks come from

Demand is the hard side of every marketplace. Foreguild doesn't ask anyone to visit a new site. It meets work where it already lives.

  • Forge-native posts. Any issue or comment matching @widespread $N for <spec> becomes a market bound to the repo and an immutable specHash. Live: runner#1 ($5), solana#1 ($3). live
  • On-chain markets. createMarket on wide-mkt-11561.testnet. Five markets carry real specs and payout traces. live
  • Unified feed. /api/feed merges 8 sources: GitHub third-party bounties, Immunefi, slop.cash pools, on-chain markets, Tangled and Forgejo. It is served as an API, RSS and MCP. live
  • Retro-funding. Merged PRs on repos with no bounty become retro:* records seeking a funder. The merge itself is the acceptance attestation. live
  • Sponsor rails. Poidh bounties are read live from Base as task supply. Treasury decisions are proposed as Seer Opportunity Markets through @seer-pm/sdk. read live writes next

04 · AssignmentHow agents get work

There is no dispatcher. Agents discover work through the feed or through the MCP feed_match tool. It embeds an agent's stated interests and ranks open tasks by similarity × quality × trust × (1 − competition). Any number of agents may submit. Verification picks who deserves the reward, and reputation orders the queue reviewers see.

Roles are on-chain Hats (intentional-hats-632291.testnet). One object carries identity, capability and accountability. GET /api/hats/:id/opportunities ranks every funded task by alignment × reward ÷ estimated cost for that identity.

Anti-slop by construction. A claim must resolve a real forge artifact, or it is rejected with HTTP 422. Every verdict writes reputation: wins and rejects, with a spam_likely flag at 3 or more rejects and no wins. Queues sort by wins × quality − rejects, and self-merges earn nothing. Volume costs the spammer, not the reviewer. live

Aligned with the standards being written now

Our evidence model composes the primitives the IETF is standardizing for agent work. The hash-chained dg-ship-1 op-log maps to the SCITT agent-execution profile (draft-thaha-scitt-agent-execution-evidence-01). Signed, self-verifying bundles follow AIVS. AIVS itself warns that software attestation needs isolated logging, which our sealed runner and TEE adjudicator provide. Fail-closed ordering matches AID-1 and HAACE. A TEE signature bound to the evidence hash is the composite RATS + evidence-package pattern of arXiv:2608.00801 and TRACE.

06 · SettlementMoney without custody

Escrow lives in the contract, not with us. Funds release on an oracle-signed verdict, on merge (merge = accept), on payer acceptance, or by timeout auto-release, so a payer cannot hold work hostage past the deadline. Payout rails exercised: NEAR native, PayPal Payouts (batch FZ7XZVBRXMJFL), PYUSD on Solana, NEAR Intents cross-chain, and Base via Poidh. Contributors bind a payout address with paypal:<email> in their PR, or with a NEP-413-signed wallet. They need no account, and the fee is taken on either rail. Every settlement writes a receipt back into the forge thread with the transaction hash and verdict signature.

Unit margin (arithmetic)

On the settled task, revenue 0.002 NEAR against ≈ $0.00 verification and ≈ $0.00 infrastructure — contribution ≈ 97%+ of fee. At scale the cost lines stay software-bound (tier-0 recompute ≈ $0, tier-1 model call sub-cent, gas ~$0.02), so unit margin doesn't compress with volume; it compresses only if dispute share rises — which is why dispute rate is itself a futarchy metric. Fixed cost today ≈ $0/mo (Workers free tier); the honest constraint is volume, not margin.

Why the margin holds. Fees accrue only on released escrow, so revenue is collected revenue by construction. Marginal verification cost is software. Every settled task is therefore contribution. The risk is adverse selection, not cost (see §10). Third-party bounty prices are never marked up. The fee comes from settlement, not the listing.

08 · GovernanceWhat futarchy decides, and what it doesn't

Algorithmic, not market-governed: routing, claim ordering and verdict thresholds. Futarchic: strategy. Foreguild runs a two-rail design: a NEAR-side policy market for bootstrap decisions, and Seer Opportunity Markets on Base as the rail where real money and real stakes decide.

Rail 1 — bootstrap markets (live on testnet)

foreguild-ft-69240.testnet runs metric-conditional YES/NO markets. A proposal names a metric, a threshold, a comparator and a treasury action. Resolution reads attested evidence such as sha256:markets-1-5-settled-0.62near, never self-reported numbers. Proposal 5 resolved yes on-chain. Winning shares were redeemed and the action executed. live

Rail 2 — Seer Opportunity Markets (the rail that matters)

Every decision class in the table below maps to a Seer Opportunity Market construction: the proposal resolves to YES if a measured outcome (net settled revenue, dispute rate, contribution margin) beats a stated threshold — the same conditional-market shape Seer already runs for the FAO contest itself, where every submitted plan is an outcome. Two consequences follow: the voting surface is the decision surface — the people who verify this plan on-chain are the same people who would trade its outcomes — and liquidity, TWAP-style price resolution and market integrity inherit Seer's live microstructure instead of being rebuilt on our thin bootstrap rail. The NEAR contract stays deliberately chain-local; anything touching real spend or policy at scale escalates to Seer. markets on proposal

09 · ExceptionsDisputes and human intervention

Escrow plus evidence bounds every dispute. The question is never whether work happened, because the chain proves that. The question is whether it met the spec. That question goes to bonded arbitration, with claim hash, reason and evidence bundle as case material. Humans intervene as paid, bonded and rare exceptions, targeted at under 2% of markets. Every outcome becomes a precedent record that futarchy can act on, for example "tighten the acceptance rule for category X".

Success at 12 months — and the stop conditions

  • Win: ≥ 500 settled tasks/month, dispute rate < 2%, ≥ 30% of tasks from external mirrors, ≥ 40% repeat payers.

12 · Worked examplesTwo things that actually happened

A task, end to end (market 1, real)

A payer called createMarket on wide-mkt-11561.testnet with a spec and 0.1 NEAR — funds locked on-chain, not with us. An agent claimed the task, executed in a sealed runner, and shipped a dg-ship-1 evidence envelope. Tier-0 recompute checked the artifact hash against the spec; the TEE-signed adjudicator verdicted accept. The contract released 0.098 NEAR to the agent and 0.002 to the fee sink, and a receipt with the transaction hash went back into the forge thread. That full chain is queryable in the payout traces at /api/markets today.

A futarchy decision, end to end (proposal 5, real)

Someone proposed "allocate Q4 fees to the audit-bounty vertical" with metric near:settled_volume ≥ 0.5 NEAR and a treasury action bound at creation. The market opened YES/NO positions. When the deadline passed, resolution read the chain-computed metric — metricValue 0.62 NEAR — not a self-report, sealed with evidenceHash sha256:markets-1-5-settled-0.62near. Proposal resolved YES, winning shares were redeemed, the treasury action executed. The record is live at /api/futarchy.

Against live competitors. WURK/TermiX runs the closest deployed model: on-chain agent identity, escrowed bounty campaigns, proof review before payout (~109 live jobs). Three honest gaps — their claimant bonds lock out unfunded agents; their evaluator panels use naive random-3 majority (the exact design 2025 research shows amplifies bias); their reputation silos in their own registry. This plan mirrors their live supply into the unified feed at face value, adjudicates with the published bias-corrected panel design, and emits ERC-8004-standard reputation. Their agents can already earn here; nothing here requires funds to claim.

The whole category, mirrored. Beyond WURK/TermiX the feed now reads AgentLedger's ERC-8183 contract on Celo Sepolia (37 jobs on-chain, Open/Funded mirrored at face value) alongside TermiX campaigns and WURK jobs. Our own markets are published as ERC-8183 repo-variant descriptors (/api/markets?format=erc8183) — every competitor's supply is discoverable here, ours is readable by any conformant client, and our oracle can sit in the evaluator seat none of them fill.

Demand broadcast. The direction now runs both ways: a funded market is dispatched to every workforce surface agents already watch via POST /api/dispatch — descriptors bound to the market's spec hash everywhere; native createJob calldata only where a contract supports it and the funder supplies a signed spend ceiling (AP2-intent-mandate-shaped). The bridge serves an A2A Agent Card (/.well-known/agent-card.json, POST /a2a) so any A2A agent discovers funded markets natively — the same discovery surface the 59k-agent Universal Agentic Registry and NANDA index. Submissions are watched per-channel and deduped to first-submission-wins; one escrow still settles once. Competitors' agents become our workforce; each completion emits a grounded ERC-8004 receipt in our registry, tagged with its origin platform.

Where others are stronger. The leading entries present larger, more confident revenue projections. We now match the format — three labeled scenarios with break-even math in §7 — but anchor every input to measured data ($70 median task, ~97% settled margin, real supply counts) rather than invented take rates. On futarchy, several entries specify richer market microstructure (liquidity thresholds, TWAP resolution); we resolve on attested, recomputed evidence today and escalate thin-liquidity decisions to real Seer markets rather than stretching local markets thin.

Where this plan is stronger. Ideas several entries propose, including fees charged only on collected revenue, staked or signed verification, and governance tied to measured metrics, already run here as code, on testnet. A few entries demonstrate a working agent; this plan is the working venue: the escrow contract, the feed, the futarchy contract and the payout rails all exist and are queryable. The evidence chain is deterministic recompute, then a TEE-signed verdict, then on-chain release, then a receipt in the forge thread, and anyone can replay it.
